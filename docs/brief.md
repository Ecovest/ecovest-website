# Source 2: *EcoVest PRD 1.0* (8 May 2026, 27 pages)

**Company:** EcoVest Technologies Ltd., Nigeria. **Tagline:** "Building Financial Intelligence."

**What it is:** an AI-powered financial growth ecosystem that helps people **learn, earn, invest and build long-term wealth** through education, technology and community. Mission: close the gap between *knowing* about money and *acting* on it, for the next generation of Africans. Launching in Nigeria, then Ghana, Kenya, South Africa and the rest of Africa.

**Vision:** Africa's most trusted financial growth platform.

## Problem (young Africans)
Little or no financial education; don't understand investing; start too late; can't tell which investments are legit; no personalised guidance; poor saving habits; **fall victim to scams**; think investing is only for the rich.
Key line: investment apps assume you already know finance, and education platforms never lead to action. **The gap between learning and doing.**

## Solution: 4 pillars
Financial Education · AI Financial Assistant · Community Learning · Investing. Takes users from beginner to confident investor, step by step, without the jargon.

## Value proposition
Doesn't just help you invest. It teaches **why, what, when, how to manage risk, and how to stay disciplined**, in one app instead of several disconnected ones.

## Audience
University students, **NYSC members**, young professionals, first-time investors, young entrepreneurs. Also freelancers, creators and small business owners. Pain points: fear of losing money, can't find trustworthy advice, no clear learning path, low income and budgeting struggles.
Personas: a 20-year-old student on **₦20,000/month** who doesn't know where to start; a 26-year-old salaried professional who wants portfolio guidance.

## Product suite (long-term)
EcoVest (flagship) · EcoVest AI · EcoVest Academy · EcoBot · EcoVest Invest · EcoVest Trade · EcoVest Quant · EcoVest Skills · EcoVest Community · EcoVest Capital.

## Core features
1. **Academy:** Personal Finance, Investing, Trading, Wall Street & Financial Analysis, FinTech & Quant, Digital Skills. Taught through modules, videos, quizzes, case studies and simulations.
2. **AI Financial Coach:** plain-language answers and personal guidance.
3. **Community Hub:** discussions, questions, expert sessions, events.
4. **Investment Discovery:** stocks, ETFs, mutual funds, bonds, *sustainable investment opportunities*.
5. Goals Tracker · 6. Budget Planner · 7. Financial News · 8. **Investment Simulator** (practise without real money) · 9. Certificates · 10. Premium.

## User journey
Download → Sign up → Onboarding quiz → Personal learning path → Lessons → Chat with EcoVest AI → Join community → Set goals → Practise investing → Start investing → Track growth.

## Business model: freemium
Free: basic courses, community, limited AI chat, news. Premium: advanced courses, unlimited AI, webinars, investment analysis, portfolio insights. Also: affiliate commissions from regulated partners, sponsored content (clearly labelled), corporate and university programmes, certifications, B2B.

## Competitors
Direct: Risevest, Bamboo, Trove, PiggyVest, Cowrywise. Indirect: Coursera, Udemy, YouTube, finance influencers, traditional advisers.
Difference: each competitor does one thing (invest, save, learn or advise). EcoVest does all four, with AI and community added.

## Roadmap (PRD version, 5 phases)
1. Community, Academy, AI Chat, Budget Tracker
2. Investment Simulator, News, Goals
3. EcoVest Invest, Portfolio Tracking
4. EcoVest Trade, EcoVest Quant
5. EcoVest Skills, EcoVest Capital

## Goals
1–2 years: active community, MVP, **10,000+ users**, launch the AI Coach, university partnerships.
5–10 years: Africa's leading financial education platform and regulated investing.

## Constraints that affect the site
- **Mobile-first; Android first, then iOS.** App store CTAs should read "Android first".
- Compliance: clear **investment risk disclosures**; investing only **through licensed partners**; KYC/AML. The site must not promise returns.
- Launch plan: beta testing, **university ambassadors, referral programme**, webinars, partnerships. These are all possible landing page sections or CTAs.

# Where the two sources disagree (needs a decision)
| | Profile deck | PRD |

**My read:** the PRD is the product truth and the deck is the brand story. The landing page could lead with the PRD's promise (learn → plan → practise → invest, for young Africans) and use the deck's community-first, sustainability-flavoured identity as the tone. The site should be honest that **Community is live now** and the rest is on the way. Don't put 10 sub-products on the homepage.

# Design direction (decided)

**EcoVest in one line:** Learn money properly, practise without risk, and invest when you're ready, with an AI coach and a community behind you. Built for young Africans, starting in Nigeria.

**Direction: Calm & premium.** References: [Public.com](https://public.com), [Wealthsimple](https://www.wealthsimple.com). Also looked at [Cowrywise](https://cowrywise.com), [Rise](https://www.risevest.com), [Brilliant](https://brilliant.org), [Duolingo](https://www.duolingo.com) and [Tomorrow Bank](https://www.tomorrow.one).
- White page, generous space, one big confident headline (Familjen Grotesk).
- The real product is the hero: a large app view showing a lesson, a ₦ savings goal and the AI coach.
- Green is an accent (buttons, highlights), not the background.
- Sections stay calm; motion is subtle and purposeful, never decorative noise.
- Trust signals (licensed partners, risk disclosure, real community) matter more than effects.
